Setting Up Your Samsung Printer

Samsung offers a range of reliable laser and inkjet printers for home and office use. Setting up your Samsung printer correctly ensures optimal print quality, reliable connectivity, and long-term performance. This guide covers everything from unboxing to driver installation and network setup.

Whether you're connecting via USB or Wi‑Fi, the process is straightforward if you follow the right steps. We'll also cover how to find the correct drivers for your specific model and what to do if your printer isn't detected.

Step 1: Unbox and Hardware Setup

Start by carefully unpacking your Samsung printer and preparing it for use. Follow these basic steps:

  • Remove all packaging – Take the printer out of the box and remove all protective tapes, foam, and shipping materials.
  • Insert the paper input tray – Slide the paper tray into the printer and adjust the paper guides for your paper size.
  • Install toner/ink cartridges – Open the front or top cover and install the toner or ink cartridges according to the color-coded guides in the printer. Remove any protective tape from the cartridges before installing.
  • Connect the power cable – Plug the power cable into the printer and connect the other end to a wall outlet. Do not plug in the USB cable until prompted during software installation.
  • Turn on the printer – Press the power button. Set the date, time, and regional settings using the printer's control panel.

Step 2: Download and Install Drivers

Before connecting the printer to your computer, you need to install the correct drivers. Samsung recommends using the most up‑to‑date drivers available on their support website.

Finding the Right Driver

To get the correct drivers for your specific printer model:

1 Visit the Samsung Support website – Go to the official Samsung support site and navigate to the "Downloads" or "Drivers" section.
2 Enter your printer model – Type your printer model number into the search box.
3 Select your operating system – Choose your OS (Windows, macOS, or Linux) from the dropdown menu.
4 Download the printer driver – Look for the file labeled "Printer Driver" or "Print Driver." Download and run the installer as administrator.
⚠️ Important: Using a driver that is not specifically designed for your printer model can cause unexpected errors or compatibility issues. Always download the dedicated driver for your model.

Step 3: Connect the Printer

USB Connection

For first-time wireless setup, Samsung recommends initially connecting the printer via USB to configure wireless settings.

  • Run the downloaded driver installer.
  • When prompted, connect the USB cable to both the printer and your computer.
  • Follow the on‑screen instructions to complete the installation.

Wireless (Wi‑Fi) Connection

Setting up a wireless connection can be done using the printer driver installer or the Samsung Easy Wireless Setup (EWS) program.

  • Using the driver installer: During installation, select "Wireless" as the connection type and follow the prompts to select your Wi‑Fi network and enter the password.
  • Using Samsung Easy Wireless Setup: If the driver is already installed, you can download and run the EWS program to configure wireless settings separately.

For users of the Samsung Xpress M2020W series, connecting via USB first and using Samsung Easy Printer Manager to configure wireless settings is an effective method.

Step 4: Print a Test Page

Once installation is complete, print a test page to confirm everything is working correctly:

  • On Windows, go to "Devices and Printers" in Control Panel.
  • Right‑click your Samsung printer and select "Printer Properties."
  • Click "Print Test Page."

If the test page prints successfully, your Samsung printer is set up and ready to use.

Troubleshooting Common Setup Issues

🔌 Printer not detected

Ensure the USB cable is securely connected and you've waited until prompted by the installer to connect it. Try a different USB port or cable. For wireless, ensure your printer is on the same network as your computer.

📶 Wi‑Fi connection fails

Check that your router is broadcasting a 2.4 GHz signal. Use the USB-based configuration method first to send Wi‑Fi settings to the printer. Restart both the printer and router.

💻 Driver installation fails

Uninstall any previously installed Samsung printer drivers before reinstalling. Run the installer as administrator and temporarily disable antivirus software during installation.

⚠️ "INF not found" error

If you encounter an "INF not found" error, try manually installing the driver through Device Manager by browsing to the folder containing the INF files.

🛠️ Pro tip: For macOS users, you can use the built-in AirPrint feature for wireless printing without installing a separate driver. For Linux users, Samsung provides specific driver packages for some models.

Important: Samsung Printer Support Transition

Please note that Samsung no longer manufactures or provides direct support for printers. All Samsung printer-related support, including drivers and downloads, is now handled by HP. For further assistance or driver downloads, please visit the official HP website.

Conclusion – Start Printing

Setting up your Samsung printer is a straightforward process when you follow the correct steps. From unboxing and ink installation to driver download and network configuration, each stage is designed to get you printing quickly and reliably.

If you encounter issues, the Samsung support website and community forums offer comprehensive guides and troubleshooting advice. Keep your drivers updated and perform regular maintenance to ensure long‑term performance.
